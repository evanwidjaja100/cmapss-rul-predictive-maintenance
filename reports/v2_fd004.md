# Phase V2-11 — FD004 generalization study

**Date:** 2026-08-15 · **Commit:** `27a3e64` · **Model:** GRU w45 huber (exact V2-4/V2-5 recipe)

## 1. Goal

Replicate the Methodology V2 pipeline on FD004 — the multi-condition C-MAPSS
dataset (6 operating conditions) — and measure whether the FD001-validated
recipe (raw-RUL target, engine-level 70/15/15 split, train-only scaler,
GRU w45 huber, batch 256, patience 8, seed 42) transfers to a harder dataset.

## 4. Results

**Validation manifest (185 rows):** RMSE 77.2262 · MAE 68.9845 · R² −2.2274 · NASA 2,951,471.82

**Official FD004 test, 248 engines (first label read):** RMSE 64.4158 · MAE 54.0058 · R² −0.3958 · NASA 2,663,846.31 (NASA mean/engine 10,741.32)

### 4.1 The model collapses to a constant

- Official predictions: mean 120.85, **std 0.00** — a degenerate constant
  output (zero variance; Pearson correlation undefined).
- Sanity vs baselines (same pipeline, same manifest):
  - mean baseline, official: RMSE 73.00 · MAE 61.01 · R² −0.79 · NASA 11,057,351.66
  - mean baseline, validation: RMSE 89.41 · MAE 81.20
- The GRU is only marginally better than predicting a constant and is
  strictly **worse than predicting the training mean** in R² terms: its
  collapsed output (120.85) sits above the official truth mean (86.55).
- Metrics are self-consistent (verified by recomputation from the saved
  prediction CSV with the project's own `rmse`/`r2`).

### 4.2 Why: multi-condition heterogeneity

- FD004 = 6 operating conditions (NASA documentation): `setting_2` spans ≥5
  discrete levels (≈0, 0.25, 0.62, 0.7, 0.84) × `setting_3` ∈ {60, 100}; all
  conditions present in both train and test. FD001 settings are essentially
  constant (single condition).
- The recipe's single global z-score scaler + one GRU has **no condition
  conditioning**: sensor dynamics are bimodal across conditions, so the
  degradation signal is drowned and early stopping settles on the constant
  that minimizes huber loss.
- Notably, FD004 train min lifetime is 128 cycles — the short-lifetime OOD
  mechanism that dominates FD001 errors (V2-6) does **not** explain this
  collapse; the collapse occurs even in-range.

## 5. Verdict

The V2 raw-RUL methodology is validated on **single-condition FD001 only**.
With default preprocessing it does **not** transfer to multi-condition FD004:
the GRU collapses to a constant and R² is negative on both the validation
manifest and the official test. No claim of FD004 competence is made.

## 6. Future work (not run — explicitly out of scope for V2-11)

- Condition-aware preprocessing: per-condition z-scoring (6 conditions) or a
  condition embedding concatenated at every time step; alternatively a
  per-condition ensemble or condition-augmented targets.
- Re-run conformal / error analysis on FD004 only after a condition-aware
  recipe produces non-degenerate predictions (V2-8 machinery is reusable
  as-is via the calibration manifest generated here: 185 rows).
